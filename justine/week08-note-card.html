My Note Card

After the lesson — for you to use on your own

Type keywords only: three to five per part, no full sentences. Your answers stay on this page and are not saved when you close it, so copy them onto paper or your phone. Then say each part out loud without reading, more than once.

1. Introduce the document

Who made it? What is it? When? Where have I seen it?

2. What it represents — and the link to the theme

What does it show? Which idea about expressing or building yourself? Which detail proves it?

3. Why I chose it

What does it mean to me? What do I notice that other people might not?
