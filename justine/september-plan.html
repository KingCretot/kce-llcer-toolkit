Justine · Mondays, 60 minutes · Sep 7, 14, 21, 28

The actual ask, from Sophie: Justine understands English fine — she watches series daily. The block is producing it live, without retreating to something written out or memorized first. Every tool below is chosen for that reason, not because it's a nice activity.
What we now know: Justine has already read roughly the first third of Brooklyn on her own — from the beginning, on her teacher's recommendation, not required. That's real content she's never spoken about out loud.

What we still don't know: her class's official syllabus, teaching order, or pace. We won't know that until after her first day back — Sep 7, the same day as Week 1. September's plan uses what she's already read as real speaking material regardless of school's pace, since that's supplementary to — not dependent on — what happens in class.
