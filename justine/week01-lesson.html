What to Expect
1

Your accent is not a problem. Sounding French is completely fine — as long as you're understood.

2

No rushing. We take everything one piece at a time.

3

Mistakes during practice are the whole point. Talking matters more than talking perfectly.

4

By June, you'll have done this so many times, nothing in that room will feel new.
