What to Check the Retelling Against

Does the student get the "why" right, not just the "what"? Plot recall is easy. Motivation and cause are what actually matter for the exam.

Does the student mention how the character feels, or just what happens to them? Emotional ambivalence is usually the real substance of a section.

Does the student connect this section to what follows? A turning point should feel like a hinge, not an isolated scene.
